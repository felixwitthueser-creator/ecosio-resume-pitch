My view of the role
A mandate is a deadline
for the customer.
For me, it's a reason to call.
Most expansion conversations need a hook. In e-invoicing, regulators write them for you: Germany in January 2027, then the next country, then the next. Every customer has a calendar of obligations, whether they know it or not.

So I'd want to know that calendar for every account in my portfolio before they do. Which subsidiary is affected, which partners are already asking, who on their side owns the topic. That isn't a sales trick. It's what 99.9% retention depends on.

And when the same question comes up in five accounts, it belongs with product and marketing, not in my notes. I ran exactly that loop at Sky, and it's the part of this role I'd enjoy most.
